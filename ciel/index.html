Ciel

Ciel is a private personal AI assistant operated solely by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Its Alcyone worker uses read-only Gmail access to detect new messages, inspect limited message metadata, and assess whether the owner should be notified. It cannot send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Ciel stores operational records locally, uses a configured model provider to interpret selected Gmail metadata, and may send relevant intelligence summaries to the owner's private Discord destination.
